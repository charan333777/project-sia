# Sia QR personality mascots

The four production assets were created with the built-in image-generation workflow. The final PNGs are 512 × 512 and use transparent backgrounds.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent QR personality mascot for the Sia web app
Input images: Image 1: exact soft 3D/clay mascot style and finish anchor; Images 2 and 3: supporting character references only.
Style/medium: polished friendly soft 3D clay illustration, rounded forms, delicate pastel shading, subtle tactile texture, premium approachable SaaS character design.
Composition/framing: one centered front-facing head-and-upper-neck mascot, symmetrical, square canvas, generous transparent padding, strong readable silhouette at 44–96 px.
Lighting/mood: soft even studio light, welcoming and calm.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with preserved alpha; one character only; no circular badge, no frame, no text, no logo, no QR code, no shadow outside the character, no watermark; consistent visual family with the baby elephant reference.
```

## Character variations

```text
Puppy: a sweet puppy mascot with cream fur, warm caramel floppy ears, small black eyes, tiny smiling mouth, and subtle peach-pink cheeks. Avoid body, collar, accessories, realistic fur, open mouth, tongue, and extra limbs.

Elephant: a sweet baby elephant mascot with powder-blue head, wide rounded ears with pale lilac inner ears, small curled trunk, small black eyes, and subtle peach-pink cheeks. Avoid body, tusks, accessories, realistic skin, extra trunk, and extra ears.

Panda: a sweet panda mascot with creamy white face, rounded black ears, balanced black eye patches, small black eyes, tiny smiling mouth, and subtle peach-pink cheeks. Avoid body, bamboo, accessories, realistic fur, extra ears, and extra eye patches.

Play: a playful abstract smiley mascot—one simple rounded lavender orb face with two sparkling star-shaped near-black eyes, a tiny joyful smile, and subtle peach-pink cheeks. Avoid body, limbs, accessories, yellow emoji colour, text, and extra facial features.
```

The puppy received one background-extraction pass to replace a rendered checkerboard with genuine alpha while preserving the character.
